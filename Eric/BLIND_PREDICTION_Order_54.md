## 2. Research methodology used

- Applied the five requested passes to every row: entity/location, contact, stated role, internal/cross-file consistency, then channel-specific actionability. Compared all 40 ListForge fields with the 11 Apollo fields; gave special attention to email-source patterns, parent/platform attribution, cross-location contact reuse, and `RecommendedOutreach`.
- `BusinessStatus`, Google Maps, website, rating/review count and `LastReviewDate` are treated as listing/activity evidence only. A recent review does **not** establish current contact/role or a working phone; an older review does **not** prove closure. The table shows the exact review-age signal per row.
- Since the campaign’s offer and ICP are missing, role-to-offer fit is explicitly uncertain. For this prediction only, **GREEN means a reasonable first phone call to the listed local business to identify/qualify the buyer**—not permission to send a personalized email or claim an unlisted person is verified. YELLOW means verify the person, route, scope or category first. RED is reserved for a positive material problem in the recommended email/enrichment route. A phone-first campaign or a corporate-wide offer could change some classifications.
- For records without a contact, Contact/Role Status explicitly says **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE**. That is not treated as proof that the business is bad. `RecommendedOutreach` is treated as ListForge’s recommendation, not evidence the channel works.
- Confidence rates confidence in the stated classification, not a calibrated probability that a call will connect. The Apollo export’s mirrored fields are not counted as independent corroboration.
