## 2. Research methodology used

- Compared all 40 ListForge fields with all 11 Apollo fields. Matches are cross-export consistency only; provenance independence is unknown. Used supplied files as primary evidence; no external company/contact pages or phone outcomes were used. The actual offer/ICP is unspecified, so role-fit judgments assume a general restaurant-relevant B2B offer and remain conditional.
- **FCA (First-Call Actionability):** GREEN means a first call to the listed business/location is reasonable from the local listing and phone fields. YELLOW means the call is still reasonable but should qualify a material entity, parent/chain/platform, outlet or routing ambiguity. RED requires positive evidence that the number is wrong/disconnected or belongs to another entity; none is present here. Missing enrichment alone is not RED; YELLOW is not a do-not-call verdict.
- **NCSA (Named-Contact Sequence Actionability):** GREEN means a supplied named person, role and route are plausibly appropriate for the assumed offer. GREEN is intentionally rare by design: it requires affirmative support for identity, current role, offer relevance and a suitable route—not just a connection, senior title or working email. YELLOW labels separately identify route, role, scope and liveness uncertainty. RED requires a positive material named-role/channel mismatch for the assumed offer. Where no person is named, use **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE**; absence is not evidence the account is bad. LF-11 has an email but no mapped person/role and remains insufficient.
- **SEQ** is the conservative named-contact/revenue-critical sequence class: it generally follows NCSA, except LF-31 is SEQ RED for positive entity/platform contamination even though NCSA is INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE. For phone-first account qualification, use FCA; do not collapse the two dimensions into one unconditional score.
- A ContactPhone area-code mismatch sets NCSA to at least YELLOW while the person-route remains unconfirmed. Area codes may reflect corporate/direct, mobile, ported or VoIP routing and do not prove geography or error.
- `LastReviewDate` is binned Low (≤3 months), Medium (4–6 months), High (≥7 months); it measures only relative business-review activity, not contact or phone freshness. It may lower NCSA confidence but does not by itself change the classification. A recent date does not prove actionability; an old date does not prove a bad record.
- Confidence describes evidence strength, not a calibrated call-connect probability: A = strong evidence; B = good/minor uncertainty; C = meaningful uncertainty; D = weak/insufficient evidence.

## 6. Definition of what would count as confirmation/refutation

- **FCA:** supported only when the listed number reaches the intended business/location or establishes a route there. Wrong/disconnected number or wrong entity refutes. No answer/voicemail is inconclusive.
- **Entity:** record whether the respondent confirms the named outlet, operating entity and relevant local/central purchasing boundary. Related locations are not duplicates merely because contact details overlap. LF-31’s Toast fields are platform-enrichment evidence, not proof its local phone is invalid.
- **NCSA:** supported only when the named person is current, in the stated role, relevant to the assumed offer and reachable by a suitable route. For INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE rows, a phone-identified owner/GM may make a sequence possible; it does not verify email deliverability. Confirming a mismatched ContactPhone reaches the named person resolves routing only, not role or offer fit.
